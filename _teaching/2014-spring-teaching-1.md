In the Spring of 2023, I taught Special Topics in Computing: Advanced Algorithms, a four-credit computing course, with my classmates Gati Aher and Sam Coleman at Olin College of Engineering. Under the auspices of Olin professors [Paul Ruvolo, Ph.D.](https://www.olin.edu/bios/paul-ruvolo), and [Rob Martello, Ph.D](https://www.olin.edu/bios/robert-martello), we created and delivered lectures to a class of 15 students on course content including network flows, heuristic algorithms, genetic algorithms, linear/integer programming, NP-completeness, and approximation algorithms.
