In Spring 2023, I was a course assistant for [K-12 Outreach: Math and Engineering for Everyone (CIE2223M)](https://www.olin.edu/articles/story-new-course-focuses-k-12-outreach-community-impact), a four- to eight-credit course offered by [Sarah Spence Adams, Ph.D.](https://www.olin.edu/bios/sarah-s-adams) at Olin College of Engineering.

Further Information
======
The course centered around designing and then running workshops to get children interested in science and math, and culminated in two separate elementary schools taking their students on a field trip to Olin's campus, where the Olin students had designed interactive educational play experiences for them. I acted as an advisor to the students, helping to anticipate potential pitfalls of play experiences and offering constructive criticism of teaching techniques (think: "don't just tell the kids about how air resistence works and drop a crumpled up piece of paper and a flat piece of paper as a demonstration, go see if Professor Lee will let you borrow his wind tunnel and have them drop stuff in it!"). I also helped to facilitate play experiences on site at at Olin during the final event and at elementary schools during the preliminary workshops. I THOROUGHLY enjoyed this experience!
